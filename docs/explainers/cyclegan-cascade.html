Paired → unpaired cascade · feature cyclegan-cascade

pix2pix for the content, CycleGAN for the look

The professor's multi-stage idea in two steps. Stage 1 is the pix2pix we already have, trained on aligned thermal/visible pairs. Stage 2 is a CycleGAN, trained with unpaired losses to move stage 1's output closer to real optical photos. In the loop arm, a frozen detector also trains stage 2.

codeshipped, 6 of 6 tasks runsfrom 2026-11-14, one arm per A100 scopepaper 2, no TIM claim

The first cascade attempt put a frozen, zero-shot enhancer (PiSA-SR) in stage 2. It failed the faithfulness gate, passing 2 of 14 tests (record 035). That was not what the professor asked for. This attempt trains stage 2.

PART 1

The pipeline, end to end

Plan Q4 · Q8

Stage 1 is a dataset, not a network

Stage 1 stays frozen, so its output never changes. We run t2o export --paired once per arm on that arm's pix2pix checkpoint. It writes every train and val translation, copies the real visible frame beside it, mirrors the labels and writes a fresh data.yaml. To stage 2, that tree is just another dataset. No composite model, no second checkpoint to load.

ADR-01

Three channels, not one

The loader always read the thermal side as one grey channel. Left as is, CycleGAN would see only the brightness of pix2pix's colour output and could not build on it. infrared_channels: 3 reads it as RGB. Only cyclegan accepts 3; every other backbone is refused at startup, and the default of 1 leaves every existing config's hash unchanged.

PART 2

Inside one CycleGAN training step

CycleGAN learns two translators at once. G_AB turns stage-1 output (domain A) into optical (domain B); it is the one that ships, since translate() is G_AB. G_BA goes back the other way and exists only to check G_AB. Two critics judge realism: D_B asks "is this a real photo?", D_A asks the same about stage-1 output.

Pick a loss term to see which path it trains.

PART 3

Unpaired on train, paired on val

The custom set does have aligned pairs. Stage 2 just never learns which photo belongs to which input. On the train split, item i loads its visible target from one fixed, seeded shuffle of the split's own photos. The input, its labels and its name stay together, so the loop arm's detection loss still scores the right boxes.

The val split stays paired. LPIPS and the faithfulness counts need each output's true photo as a reference.

PART 4

How strong the detector's push is, and for how long

House rule since record 006: a new backbone gets a short loss_share.py probe before its campaign, and the detection term must be 20–30% of the objective. Too low and the loop arm is a second control. Too high and the detector's gradient beats the cycle term and paints adversarial texture.

Try it

Detection share vs. grad_scale

grad_scale0.60
projected share26.4%
in 20–30% bandyes
share = 2.28·g / (2.28·g + 3.81)

From the probe at 0.15: raw loss_det ≈ 2.28, and GAN + cycle + identity = 3.81. This is a straight-line projection. A stronger dose drives the raw detection loss down, so the share a run reaches tends to land below it (record 006, F29). That is why 0.6 sits in the upper half of the band. A loss_share.py read after the run reports what it actually reached.

Schedule

Learning rate over 200 epochs

  • Upstream CycleGAN's schedule: hold, then decay to zero. Stepped per batch, and saved in the checkpoint, so a resume continues the decay.
  • Batch 1, as upstream: 600 frames × 200 epochs ≈ 120k updates. At E3's batch of 8 it would be only ~15k.
  • The schedule always starts decaying halfway. So the 50-epoch probe was read over its first 25 epochs, the stretch that matches the campaign's constant-rate start.

Caveat · plan Q5

The arms differ in two places

Each arm reads its own stage 1, so control is detector-free end to end and loop has a detector in both stages. That tells the cleanest "detector everywhere vs. nowhere" story, but stage 2's share of any gap cannot be separated out.

Caveat · plan Q1, Q3

One seed per arm

A reading, not a measurement: no significance test. One arm per card breaks record 007's split-by-seed rule, but with one seed there is no paired test for a GPU difference to bias. The record states it.

Caveat · stage-1 export

Trained in-sample, scored out-of-sample

Stage 2 trains on pix2pix's outputs for train frames, which pix2pix itself was trained on. It is scored on val outputs, which pix2pix never saw and which may be rougher than anything stage 2 learned from.

When both runs finish

  1. t2o aggregate on zero-shot mAP@50 (read by the independent yolo11s judge) and LPIPS against the real val photos.
  2. t2o faithfulness for invented and erased objects, the test PiSA-SR failed.
  3. loss_share.py on the loop run, over all 200 epochs and over the first 25, to report the dose it really ran at.
  4. One /log-experiment record that states all three caveats above.

Whatever it shows stays in paper 2. Entering the TIM paper would need a separate gate feature with faithfulness margins fixed before the first score, by 2027-01-10.
